Saved playlists

Playlists saved from the CommercialBrainz tab write matching spots (title, YouTube link, duration) into the Commercials table so breaks do not call CommercialBrainz. At midnight ChannelFlow refreshes those stored links. Click Pull to refresh a playlist now.

CommercialBrainz YouTube Pool

Fill in the filters, then Save to playlist. ChannelFlow copies matching CommercialBrainz metadata and YouTube links into the Commercials table (one pull per save, not during live breaks). At midnight those stored links are refreshed. Assign playlists to a channel on the Commercials tab. Paste a YouTube cookies.txt on the YouTube tab to use your account (including Premium when the cookie is from a Premium login). SponsorBlock skips in-video sponsors during playback.

YouTube

ChannelFlow streams CommercialBrainz spots from YouTube at break time (no local download). yt-dlp already omits YouTube pre-roll ads. Paste a Netscape cookies.txt from a signed-in browser to use your YouTube account. If that account has YouTube Premium, yt-dlp can request Premium formats. ChannelFlow cannot turn Premium on by itself.

Emergency Broadcast System

When NOAA has watches or warnings for the default Weather tab location, TV, movies, and music play Weather/weather_alert.ogg over the current picture (or the alerts screen), then the full-screen alerts or the bottom bar, then Weather/weather_alert_single.mp3 at the end. The picture stays up — there is no black screen. Each alert dumps the run-ahead buffer down to 60 seconds so it reaches the tuner faster, then the buffer fills back to the Transcode setting. Another alert dumps it again. WeatherStar and FlowWire keep their own displays.

AI Lineup Generator

AI lineup generation is disabled. Configure API keys below, then enable the switch to generate lineups.

When on, primetime that night plays catalog episodes and movies whose original air month and day match (any year). Leftover primetime stays the usual lineup. Sequential episode order is unchanged. Takes effect on the next playout rebuild.

Test Connection works before enabling AI. Enter an API key, pick the matching provider, then test. Save settings after a successful test.

Queued new channels can also be processed from Dashboard → Scheduled Tasks → ChannelFlow-Server AI Lineup Auto-Apply (Channel Add).

Weather guide metadata

At local midnight, ChannelFlow-Server pulls the real forecast from the Weather tab source (NOAA or Open-Meteo) for each weather channel location and writes that day's TV guide titles and descriptions. If AI is enabled, it turns those facts into cable-guide copy; if not, the live temperatures and conditions go in the guide as-is. Click Generate Weather Guide Cache to refresh today now. Transcode

Live MPEG-TS encoding for every ChannelFlow channel, including news and weather. Saved settings override FFMPEG_HWACCEL, FFMPEG_VIDEO_ENCODER, and FFMPEG_VAAPI_DEVICE without restarting the container. In-progress streams pick this up on the next retry.

Loading transcode status…

Pass /dev/dri into the container for Intel Quick Sync. Typical Unraid/Linux path is /dev/dri/renderD128.

Auto picks h264_vaapi, h264_nvenc, or libx264 from the acceleration setting. If the VAAPI device is missing, ChannelFlow falls back to software encode.

Lets ffmpeg encode ahead of the tuner and hold that video so a hitch or commercial switch does not freeze the picture. New tuners also start from this buffer. 0 stays live (real time). 180–300 seconds (3–5 minutes) is the right size if the picture pauses. Maximum is 600 (10 minutes).

FlowWire News

Apply the FlowWire News preset (or set a channel to content type News). ChannelFlow-Server fetches RSS, scrolls headlines with an ASS overlay, shows story photos when the feed includes them, and optionally reads them with TTS. The bulletin opens with intro music and FlowWire.png, then Catherine_Wolfe.jpg while she reads the anchor intro. Story photos play during the newscast, her photo returns for the sign-off, then outro audio plays with the logo again.

Catherine reads the anchor intro before the stories, then the anchor outro as her sign-off immediately before outro audio. Leave both blank to use her defaults. Her photo is News/Catherine_Wolfe.jpg in the ChannelFlow logo set. Bumper music comes from News/FlowWire-intro.mp3 and News/FlowWire-outro.mp3. Override those with intro.mp3 and outro.mp3 in the server news folder, or a short clip from News music is used if nothing is found.

AI TTS uses the provider, API key, and AI voice on the AI tab. If AI speech fails, ChannelFlow falls back to Google TTS.

Uses the AI tab provider to merge RSS into a single newscast presented by FlowWire News anchor Catherine Wolfe, dropping duplicate coverage of the same event. Custom anchor intro/outro still override her open and sign-off if you fill them in. She will call the show FlowWire News, not FinTV News.

Turn on Play no music at all for TTS or silence with no bed. Otherwise pick a Jellyfin music library, or leave on Off Air to use the Off Air background music setting.

News videos

The news channel plays a premade MP4 (intro + speech + story images/text + outro). ChannelFlow builds that file every 6 hours (00:00, 06:00, 12:00, 18:00 local) from unused RSS headlines. The previous video stays on air until the new file is finished and verified. If there are no new stories, or fewer than the minimum, the run is skipped.
